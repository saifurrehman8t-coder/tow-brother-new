What is the flatbed car towing cost in Lahore?

Hydraulic flatbed towing starts from Rs. 6,500 for hatchbacks and up to Rs. 9,000+ for luxury, hybrid, and EV vehicles for the first 5-10 KM. Additional KM charged at Rs. 200/km.

Why should automatic car owners use a flatbed instead of a wheel-lift?

When an automatic/CVT car is towed with two wheels on the ground, the transmission runs dry without engine lubrication, causing permanent gearbox damage worth Rs. 150,000+. A flatbed carrier lifts all 4 wheels off the road, eliminating this risk entirely.
