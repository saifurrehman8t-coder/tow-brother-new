Car Recovery & Towing Service Lahore FAQs

1. How much does car recovery and towing service cost in Lahore?

Standard wheel-lift car recovery in Lahore starts from Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 6,000 for local transfers (up to 5–10 KM), while zero-damage hydraulic flatbed towing for automatic, hybrid, and SUV vehicles typically ranges between Rs. 6,500 and Rs. 9,000 depending on total distance.

2. How quickly can your tow truck reach my breakdown location in Lahore?

Our average response time is 15 to 25 minutes. Because we station recovery trucks at Multan Road (Hanjarwal), Thokar Niaz Baig, Lahore Ring Road, and DHA, we dispatch the closest unit immediately after you share your WhatsApp location pin.

3. Should I book a hydraulic flatbed carrier or a wheel-lift car lifter?

Always book a hydraulic flatbed carrier for automatic transmission (CVT/DCT) cars, hybrid/electric cars (HEV/EV), 4x4/AWD SUVs, and cars with broken axles. Book a wheel-lift car lifter for manual/compact cars or recoveries inside narrow streets and low-ceiling basement parkings.
